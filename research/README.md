# Model research

The current public forecast uses [count M2](count/README.md) and the [21:00–08:00 mist CNN](mist/README.md). Each folder explains its target, held-out assessment, alternatives and remaining uncertainty. The [count report](../validation/research/count_model_report.html) and [mist report](../validation/research/mist_model_report.html) are retrospective ERA5 analyses, while `scripts/production/` and `model/mist/` define what runs live.

Count alternatives M3a, M3b and prior-morning rain remain research comparisons. Climatology and matched logistic are mist evaluation baselines. Historical issued forecasts and future observed counts are needed to assess the live forecast; there is no post-2023 count score or post-2013 mist-label score yet.
